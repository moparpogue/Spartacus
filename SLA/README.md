### Hello Everyone 

This is a community modification of the CN3DP Spartacus Toolhead aimed at producing SLA optimized models for print. 


### Who is this Toolhead for? 

These toolhead models are for those who wish to use their resin printer to produce extremely accurate models of the Spartacus utilizing high temp resins.  Resin typically has higher accuracy, better layer adhesion and higher rigidity at the cost of being more brittle. 

Weights are dependent on resin used. Initial tests with solid infill resulted in a 30% weight increase over the FDM models.  This modification is NOT for those who are chasing lower weights, however skeletonization and infill optimizations may negate this.

### What is different from the stock CN3DP design. 

- Thread holes are modeled into the design.  Heat set threads don't work with resin.
- Stretch goal:  Skeletonize select parts to reduce weight.

### The design goal:

- Produce component compatible version of Spartacus via SLA printing.
